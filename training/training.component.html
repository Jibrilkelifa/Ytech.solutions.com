Training

We help you educate and train your employees in best practices in IT security.

Your employee is the most critical line of defense against cyber threats and as well the irreplaceable operator of technologies and solutions deployed inside your company. It is becoming increasingly important to build the capacity of employees by providing them with continuous training required for their job.

We offer a variety of IT-related training (vendor/non-vendor focused) courses and as well security exercises designed for you as per your need. The training can either take place at our premises together with other participants or can be arranged on-site at your offices, exclusively for your employees.
